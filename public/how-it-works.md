# How Reorder works

Reorder is a restocking service with software inside it. This page follows one order from the moment
a sale is recorded to the moment replacement stock is on your shelf.

## In one sentence

Your sales tell Reorder what is running out, Reorder turns that into a priced restock order you
approve, and the goods are sourced and delivered to your business.

## Step 1 — You sell

You keep trading normally. Sales are recorded through the Reorder till, or through the POS system you
already use where it can share its daily sales. No new habits are needed at the counter.

## Step 2 — Stock updates itself

Every sale reduces the stock figure for that item. You are not asked to count the shelf at the end of
the day to find out what is missing.

## Step 3 — Reorder works out what is actually running low

This is the part that replaces the notebook. Rather than a single "low stock" threshold, Reorder looks
at:

| What it considers | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| How fast the item sells | A slow seller at 5 units is not the same as a fast seller at 5 units |
| How much is left | The starting point for the calculation |
| How long your supplier takes | If delivery takes three days, reordering on the day it finishes is too late |
| The pack size you buy in | You cannot order a third of a carton, so quantities are rounded to what can be bought |
| Shelf life, where it applies | Short-dated stock is a loss, so it is not over-ordered |
| What you sold the same time last year | Season and demand patterns, once there is enough history |

## Step 4 — You get a restock list, not a surprise

At closing time your list is ready. Each line shows the item, what you have left, the suggested
quantity, and the reason for that quantity in plain words. You can change a quantity, remove a line,
or add something that is not on it.

Nothing is ordered until you say so.

## Step 5 — Reorder prices it and sources it

Once you approve the list, Reorder confirms availability and price with our supply network —
importers, distributors and wholesalers. If something is unavailable, you are asked what you want to
do about that line. We do not silently substitute an item you did not approve.

## Step 6 — It is delivered and checked

Your order arrives within the delivery window you chose. You confirm what you received, and any
shortage, damage or wrong item is recorded at the door with a photograph. Delivered quantities go
back into your stock, so the next restock list starts from the truth.

## What you need to start

| Situation | What Reorder needs from you |
|---|---|
| You already use a POS | A way to share daily sales — an export or a read-only connection |
| You keep records on paper | A short assisted stocktake so the first list starts from a real position |
| You run a kiosk or small shop | Nothing but the free app, and a few minutes to set up your usual items |

## What Reorder does not do

- It is not a payment processor or a bank. It does not offer credit on its own account.
- It does not promise delivery in minutes. Orders follow a published schedule for your area.
- It is not an accounting package. It does not do payroll, tax filing or full bookkeeping.
- It does not sell medicines except where a properly licensed partner is the seller, and only once
  that arrangement is in place.

## Fair questions about medicines

Pharmaceutical supply in Nigeria is a licensed activity, tied to registered premises and named
professionals. Reorder does not treat medicine as ordinary goods. Where a pharmacy uses Reorder, the
licensed pharmacy remains the buyer and the seller of record, and Reorder provides the restock
list, sourcing coordination and delivery as a service. We will only move medicines under a structure
that satisfies the regulator, and we would rather say that plainly than blur it.
