# Frequently asked questions

## What is Reorder, in one paragraph?

Reorder is a restocking service for retail businesses. Your sales are recorded, the system works out
what is running low, and at the end of the day you get a priced restock order to approve. We source
the goods from importers, distributors and wholesalers, and deliver them to your business. The point
is that you stop closing the shop to go and buy stock.

## Do I have to use the Reorder POS?

No. If you already use a POS or inventory system, we only need a way to see your daily sales —
typically an export file or a read-only connection. If you keep records on paper, we do an assisted
count with you so the first restock list starts from a real position.

## What does it cost?

There is no charge for the software for small shops and informal retailers. Revenue comes from the
goods we supply and from a delivery charge. The delivery charge is published for your area rather
than quoted per trip, so you know what a delivery costs before you order — and you can compare it
directly with what a market trip costs you today.

## Where do you operate?

Reorder is starting with a single pilot area rather than across the country. Restocking only works
economically when deliveries are clustered, so we open an area, serve it properly, and then expand.
Contact us with your location and we will tell you honestly whether you are inside the current area
or on the list for the next one.

## How is this different from ordering on an app from a wholesaler?

An ordering app waits for you to decide what you want. Reorder works from what you actually sold, so
the decision is largely made for you, and the quantity is calculated rather than guessed. It also
bundles several suppliers into one order and one delivery, which a single wholesaler cannot do.

## Will you substitute items I did not approve?

No. If a line is unavailable, you are asked what you want to do. Nothing reaches your door that you
did not approve, and quantities are checked with you at delivery.

## What about expiry dates?

For dated goods we agree a minimum remaining shelf life with each supplier and check it when the
order is picked. Short-dated stock is not something we pass on to you and then argue about.

## Do you sell medicines?

Only where it is done properly. Pharmaceutical supply in Nigeria is licensed and tied to registered
premises and named professionals, so Reorder does not treat medicine as ordinary goods. Where a
pharmacy uses Reorder, the licensed pharmacy remains the buyer and seller of record and Reorder
provides the restock list, sourcing coordination and delivery as a service.

## Do you offer credit?

Not on our own account. Some businesses buy on credit from a wholesaler they have known for years,
and we are not going to pretend that is easy to replace. Our starting position is pay on delivery or
pay before dispatch, and we would rather earn the order history first.

## What happens if something arrives damaged or short?

You record it at the door with a photograph and the quantity. That becomes a claim against the
supplier, and the credit is posted against your account rather than left as an argument. This is why
we ask for photographic evidence — it settles disputes in hours instead of weeks.

## Can I use Reorder for a shop that is not a pharmacy or a supermarket?

Yes. Kiosks, table-top traders and provisions shops are a core part of what Reorder is for. For those
businesses the app is free: record your sales, see your stock, order what has finished, and we bring
it to your shop.

## Can I still go to the market myself?

Of course. Reorder is meant to replace the routine trip, not to stop you from buying specialty items
yourself. In practice most businesses start by moving a share of their restocking across, then move
more once they see the restock list is right.

## How do I start?

Send us a message with what you sell and roughly how often you restock. We will tell you whether you
are in a current area, and if you are, we will set up your first order together.
